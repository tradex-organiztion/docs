## Advanced Chart Tools & Library

Real-time market data and professional chart tools.

<Frame>
  ![Charts 1](/images/차트_1.png)
</Frame>

- **110\+ smart drawing tools**: Add geometric shapes to highlight important spots on your chart, run measurements and projections, or use comments, tables, and emojis.
- **400\+ indicators & strategies**: Indicators visualize trading and financial statistics such as price levels, volume, and financial ratios.
- **Multi-timeframe**: Charts from 1-minute candles up to monthly candles.

---

## Chart Library

Save and load chart layouts and more.

<Frame>
  ![Charts 2](/images/차트_2.png)
</Frame>

- Click Save at the top right of the chart to save everything, including your drawings and indicators.
- Click the toggle next to the layout name to browse and load saved layouts.

## Trading Alert System

Price alerts, technical alerts, and multi-condition alerts.

<Frame>
  ![Charts 3](/images/차트_3.png)
</Frame>

- **Create alerts**: Click the icon at the top or right-click on the chart to set an alert.
- **Edit alerts**: Edit configured alerts from the alert list on the right.
