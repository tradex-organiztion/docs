## Home Dashboard

The home screen shows the following information in real time.

<Frame>
  ![Home](/images/홈.png)
</Frame>

- **Total assets**: Total asset value across your connected exchanges and its rate of change.
- **This month's realized PnL**: Realized profit and loss from this month's trades, and the change compared to last month.
- **7-day win rate & weekly equity curve**: Your win rate over the last 7 days and the change compared to the previous week.
- **Risk score**: Analyzes your key risk pattern metrics and shows your current status.

The inbox is where you check your Tradex notifications.
