# Profit Management

Profit management is a feature for systematically tracking and managing the profits and losses generated by your trading.

---

## Profit Dashboard

Get an at-a-glance view of your PnL by period on the profit management screen.

### Key metrics

- **Total realized PnL**: Confirmed profits and losses within the selected period
- **Unrealized PnL**: The mark-to-market PnL of currently open positions
- **Cumulative fees**: The total fees paid on your trades
- **Net profit**: Your actual profit after deducting fees

---

## Profit Calendar

See your daily profits and losses in a calendar view.

- Green: profitable days
- Red: losing days
- Color intensity: reflects the size of the PnL

Reviewing monthly patterns shows you which periods you perform best in.

---

## Equity Curve

View your cumulative PnL curve by period.

- Switch between daily / weekly / monthly
- Evaluate your trading consistency from the slope and stability of the curve

---

## Tax Report

Download a report of your yearly trading history to use for tax filing.

- Export trade history as CSV
- Yearly total profit / loss summary

> 💡 For crypto tax matters, we recommend consulting a tax professional.
