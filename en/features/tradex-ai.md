## Tradex AI Agent

Ask the Tradex AI agent anything and get information and insights directly.

<Frame>
  ![Tradex AI 1](/images/TradexAI_1.png)
</Frame>

- **Choose your AI model**: Pick between GPT, Gemini, and Claude models.
- **Ask questions**: Try questions like "What pattern did my losses cluster around last month?" or "What should I improve in this week's trades?"
- **Live market briefings**: Get economic news, current market trends, and more.
- **Technical analysis**: A trading-specific reinforcement-learning model assists with chart-based technical analysis.
- **Learns from your trades**: Learns from your trading data to deliver strategy analysis and insights tailored to you.
- **File upload & analysis**: Upload chart screenshots or other materials to include them in the analysis.

---

## Opening the Side Panel

You can summon the AI agent from any screen in Tradex.

<Frame>
  ![Side panel AI](/images/사이드탭AI.png)
</Frame>

- While using the service, click the button at the top right at any time to open the AI agent.
- If you open the AI agent from a chart screen, the AI answers based on that chart.

## Credits

Credits are the currency consumed when you use Tradex AI features.

<Info>
  Using a smarter AI model or requesting a lot of work at once may consume a few more credits.
</Info>

- Credits are consumed each time you chat with the AI agent, deducted in proportion to the amount of information processed.
- Typical tasks or conversations consume around 5–10 credits.
- Monthly credits are granted based on your billing date and reset at the end of each billing cycle.
