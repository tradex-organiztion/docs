---
title: "Trading Journal"
description: "Automatically record every trade from your connected exchanges, and manage your own analysis rationale and reviews."
---

---

<Steps>
  <Step title="STEP 1. Automatic journal entries" stepNumber={1} titleSize="h2">
    Tradex automatically detects your positions and creates a journal entry the moment you enter one.

    <Frame>
      ![Journal 2](/images/매매일지_2.png)
    </Frame>

    - **Automatic journal**: An entry is created on the position's entry date, recording the position and order details.
    - **Calendar / list view**: Browse your journal in whichever format you prefer.
    - **Manual entries**: If there's a trade you want to record separately, you can add it manually.

    <Info>
      Realized PnL, fees, quantities, and other figures may differ slightly depending on the exchange environment and exchange rates.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="STEP 2. Write a pre-trade scenario" titleSize="h2">
    Right after entering a new position, write out your pre-trade scenario.

    <Frame>
      ![Journal 3](/images/매매일지_3.png)
    </Frame>

    - **Indicators**: Select the indicators you used as the basis for this entry.
    - **Timeframe**: Select the timeframe you used for your chart analysis.
    - **Technical analysis**: Select the technical analysis setups you used as the basis for this entry.
    - **TP/SL**: Set the take-profit and stop-loss prices for this position.
    - **Entry rationale**: Freely describe why you entered this position.
    - **Target scenario**: Freely describe your target scenario and management plan for this position.
    - Fill in every field and save to complete the pre-trade scenario.
  </Step>
  <Step title="STEP 3. Edit templates" titleSize="h2">
    If the options don't include the setups you actually used for the entry, you can edit the template.

    <Frame>
      ![Journal 4](/images/매매일지_4.png)
    </Frame>

    - Click "Edit template" inside the pre-trade scenario to edit the detailed items of each strategy template.
    - Items are organized into categories and subcategories, and every item can be edited, deleted, or added.
    - Click and drag the icon on the left to reorder items.
    - Save your changes to finish editing the template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="STEP 4. Write a post-trade review" titleSize="h2">
    Right after closing a position, write your post-trade review.

    <Frame>
      ![Journal 5](/images/매매일지_5.png)
    </Frame>

    - **Chart screenshot**: Upload a chart image from the time you entered the position.
    - **Review**: Freely note what went well, what you regret, and what to improve in this trade.
    - **Principle compliance**: Check only the trading principles that applied to — and were followed in — this trade.
    - Fill in every field and save to complete the review.

    <Info>
      Your journal entries feed AI analysis and learning to provide a personalized experience.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>
