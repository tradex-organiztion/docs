## Why do trading principles matter?

Trading principles are the single most important element for managing risk and keeping your trading stable.

- Top traders work hard to consistently follow a **coherent set of trading principles**.
- But when the market moves violently or emotions kick in, sticking to those principles is extremely hard.
- Tradex detects trades that deviate from your principles, alerts you, and helps you build the habit.

---

## Registering a Trading Principle

Add your own rules under **Core Features \> Trading Principles**.

### Types of principles you can set

| Type | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Max loss limit | Daily loss no worse than -3% |
| Position size | A single position at most 10% of balance |
| Leverage limit | 10x or lower |
| Trading hours | Only trade between 9 AM and 6 PM |
| Symbol restriction | Only trade BTC and ETH |
| Consecutive loss rule | Stop trading for the day after 3 consecutive losses |

### How to register

1. Click **Trading Principles \> Add principle**
2. Choose a principle type
3. Enter the condition values
4. Save

---

## Violation Alerts

When a trade that deviates from a registered principle is detected:

- A violation notification appears in your inbox
- A push notification is sent (if notifications are enabled)
- The trade is automatically tagged **Principle violated**

---

## Compliance Rate

At the top of the Trading Principles screen, you can check your compliance rate by period.

> 💡 Analyzing the correlation between your compliance rate and your returns helps you build a better strategy.
