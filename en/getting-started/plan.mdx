<Steps>
  <Step title="STEP 1. Choose a plan" titleSize="h2">
    /image

    - Open your profile at the bottom left and go to **Account Settings → Subscription** to compare plans.
    - Select the plan you want and upgrade.
    - Plans differ by feature set and monthly credit allowance.

    <Tip>
      Choose annual billing to get an additional discount on the service.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="STEP 2. Pay" titleSize="h2">
    /image

    - Click the **Upgrade** button on the plan you want to start the checkout flow.
    - Review the final amount in the payment window, then click confirm and pay.
    - Once you register a payment method, payment completes immediately and all benefits of the new plan apply right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="STEP 3. Change or cancel a plan" titleSize="h2">
    /image

    - If you need more features and AI credits, you can upgrade to a higher plan at any time.
    - Click the **Upgrade plan** button on a plan above your current one to start the payment flow.

    /image

    - When upgrading, the price difference from your current plan is calculated and shown; click **Confirm and pay** to pay the difference immediately.
    - From the moment payment completes, the new plan's credit limit and all paid features apply immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Credits

Credits are the currency consumed when you use Tradex AI features.

- Credits are consumed each time you chat with the AI agent, deducted in proportion to the amount of information processed.
- Typical tasks or conversations consume around 5–10 credits.
- Monthly credits are granted based on your billing date and reset at the end of each billing cycle.

<Info>
  Using a smarter AI model or requesting a lot of work at once may consume a few more credits.
</Info>
