## Tradex Closed Beta Launch Event

After six months of detailed planning and development, Tradex launched its beta service on April 20.

<Info>
  Closed beta event period: Apr 20, 2026 – Jun 30, 2026
</Info>

- Only 100 beta testers receive a Premium plan (worth ₩30,000 per month) for free.
- Beta testers also receive one extra month of the Premium plan after the official launch.
- Note that AI credit usage may be limited during the closed beta period.
- Thank you sincerely to everyone who applied.

---

## Live Bug Report Channel

During the event period, support and inquiries are available 24 hours a day.

- If you run into any issue while using the service, report it via the chat widget at the bottom right and we'll fix it and get back to you right away.
- Users who submit detailed feedback and bug reports will receive additional benefits.
