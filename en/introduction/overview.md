## Make Your Trading Easier

Tradex is an **AI agent service for individual traders in the global crypto market**.\
It supports every stage of trading — from market and chart analysis to opening and closing positions and reviewing your trades afterward.

---

## Why was Tradex built?

There are hundreds of millions of individual traders in financial markets worldwide. The crypto market trades roughly $15 trillion per year on average, and about 70% of that volume comes from futures trading. Yet only **9%** of these traders achieve consistent profits — the remaining **91% lose money over the long run**.

Traders don't lose money because they lack knowledge of some indicator or trading technique. They lose because they **repeat the same mistakes over and over**. Traders who move enormous markets can analyze the market and the charts, but **they never analyze their own strategies and patterns**. Emotional entries, inconsistent strategies, and poor risk management lead to massive losses — and the cycle repeats every day.

To trade profitably and consistently, you need to record and review a huge amount of information for every position — entry time, symbol, size, rationale, and more — and continuously refine your strategy. But manually logging and analyzing every trade to improve your strategy is extremely inefficient, and close to impossible in practice.

**Tradex was built to remove this bottleneck.**
